# Chronicle (DC1): combat and monsters

## What this covers

How Chronicle's dungeon combat works in retail code: the player's melee, ranged and item attacks; the collision-hit records they leave behind; the damage formula applied to monsters; the damage a monster deals to the player; the monster runtime (`CMonstorUnit`, its 167-entry species table, its per-floor layouts and its spawn and activation rules); the scripted hit, guard and projectile opcodes monsters use; the reward and wear that follow a kill; and the separate "event battle" timed-prompt system (`ebattle`).

Source scope: `ps2/src` and `ps2/include` in the Chronicle checkout (master 3bc16763), with `port/src` checked for every `PORT_OVERRIDE` that touches combat. The PAL pack extracts were used only to confirm the monster script and model file naming. Community pages (`docs/fandom/Dark_Cloud.wiki`, `Weapon_abilities.wiki`, `Weapon_attributes.wiki`, `Attachments.wiki`) were used for behaviour checks only. Character identity, party storage and character model loading are covered in `c1-characters.md` and are not repeated here.

Not covered in depth: `battle_globals.cpp` (name entry and the opening book; it contains no combat code), `battlemenu.cpp` (the in-dungeon pause menu; it displays weapon stats and element/monster tables but does not decide damage), `fishing.cpp` and `fish.cpp` (the fish fight uses the ebattle state enum but is a separate minigame), and `actionseq.cpp` (cutscene actors).

## Key facts

- The retail monster damage function is `CMonstorUnit::CheckDmg`, about 570 lines. It is a member function in `ps2/src`, not overridden in `port/src`, so the PC port runs the retail logic unchanged. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1060; chronicle:port/src/monstorunit.cpp:15 (only `DrawShadowMonstor` and the table are overridden in that file)
- Monsters are a fixed table of 167 species entries, `MonstorTable[167]`, each with model files, a script name, max HP, a monster family, five elemental weights, defense, hardness, two shot-effect indices, experience, money and money chance, a kind, a steal item, a drop item set, an item-damage rate, a status chance, a rare item, six per-attacker damage percentages and a knockback scale. chronicle:ps2/include/monstorunit.hpp:182-212; chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:65
- The table breaks down into 118 normal, 29 "no lock-on" (bosses and scripted parts), 10 small mimics and 10 large mimics. By family: 47 Mage, 25 Undead, 20 Mimic, 17 Stone, 12 Beast, 12 Metal, 10 Dino, 8 Plant, 8 Sea, 8 Sky (counted from the table; family enum at chronicle:ps2/include/monstorunit.hpp:43-52). Max HP runs from 12 (Cave Bat) to 50000 (Black Knight Mount). Entries 0 to 35 (the base-game species) run from 23 to 750 HP; the "(Enhanced)" rows run to 19500.
- The port's `MonstorTable` differs from retail in one deliberate way: 16 species (base and Enhanced rows) have `drops_items` set to 1 where retail has 0, so they now drop keys, attachments, money and rare items. The port comment says so. chronicle:port/src/monstorunit.cpp:40-43; compare chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:65-
- Each dungeon's floor layout is a table of up to 9 monster species per floor, terminated by `monster_no == -1`. Seven dungeons; the back-side (`Ura`) layouts are separate arrays. Floor counts are 15, 17, 18, 18, 15, 26 and 100 (the `maxFloorTbl__3` array says 25 for the Gallery). chronicle:ps2/include/monstorunit_floor_data.inc:2-478; chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:47
- The first and third fields of each layout entry are never read by the dungeon loader. Only `monster_no` is passed on (`SetupBaseModel` at `layout->monster[monster].monster_no`). The third field sums to 100 on every populated floor of the normal layouts (206 of 209 floors; the other three are empty), and on the back-side layouts wherever a floor is populated (14 of 209 are empty). That suggests percentage weights, but no code reads them. chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:6544-6549; chronicle:ps2/include/monstorunit.hpp:559-572 (structs `BT_ENEMY_LAYOUT` and `BT_ENEMY_FLOOR`)
- Spawning is a uniform draw over the floor's loaded kinds: `selected = model_count * rand`. Fifteen monsters are placed per floor load. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:550-630 (`ArrangementPos`); chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:6487-6490
- At most four monsters are "active" (state 2, which runs scripts and takes hits) at a time. Monsters beyond the four nearest are held and marked `view_held`. The hold is skipped on the last floor (`floor + 1 == max_floor` returns early) and never applies to no-lock-on kinds. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:940-1020 (`CheckViewLevel`)
- Hits are records in a shared pool of 96 `COLLISION_HIT` entries, used by both player and monsters. `CCollisionData::Set` returns the index or, if the pool is full, falls off the end of a non-void function. chronicle:ps2/include/collisiondata.hpp:64-103, 163; chronicle:ps2/src/collisiondata.cpp:797-829
- Monster-side hits are found by `FindMonsterHit`, which is a sphere test: distance from hit position to sphere center less than or equal to the sum of the two radii, with `target_mask & 2` and `phase == ready_phase`. chronicle:ps2/include/collisiondata.hpp:146-156
- Player-side hits are found by `CheckHitUser`, which tests a horizontal radius and a vertical overlap against the character's height. Height comes from the per-character `CharaHeight` table: Toan 16, Xiao 14, Goro 16, Ruby 16, Ungaga 18, Osmond 15. chronicle:ps2/src/collisiondata.cpp:840-894; chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:52-56
- Every melee and ranged hit that the player makes is stamped in `basic_damage` (or its inline equivalent in the shot code) with the attacker's `owner` (character index 0 to 5), an `attack_no`, the weapon's monster-effectiveness table `vs_monster`, the weapon's ability bits `weapon_flags`, and the element bit from `GetWeaponElementAttr`. chronicle:ps2/src/charakey.cpp:39-52; chronicle:ps2/src/motionmodel.cpp:16-22
- Character indices come from `enum Character`: Toan 0, Xiao 1, Goro 2, Ruby 3, Ungaga 4, Osmond 5. These numbers are used as array indices in the hit record path, so the damage code assumes six slots. chronicle:ps2/include/itemdata.hpp:485-491
- Monster-owned hits use `owner = 200 + 5 * monster_index` with the sphere index in `attack_no`. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1079; chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7156 (decoding the owner back to a monster index)
- The element bits on a hit are `0x01` fire, `0x02` cold, `0x04` thunder, `0x08` wind, `0x10` holy. The element index used for weights is 0 to 4 in that order; index 5 means none. chronicle:ps2/include/collisiondata.hpp:40-47; chronicle:ps2/src/hitvalue.cpp:29 (`element_tbl`)
- Weapon ability bits (`WEAPON_HAVE::flags`, copied into `weapon_flags`) used by the damage code include `0x2` (Big Bucks: money doubled on kill), `0x4` (Poor: money halved), `0x10` (10 units of drink on a kill), `0x20` (poison chance), `0x40` (stop chance), `0x80` (steal chance), `0x400` (drain), `0x1000` (critical), `0x2000` (experience times 1.2). chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1291, 1397, 1406, 1561, 1571, 2212, 2170-2250
- Weapon wear ability bits: `0x100` (fragile, wear doubled) and `0x200` (durable, wear halved). chronicle:ps2/src/hitvalue.cpp:37-70 (`BattleSubWeaponDmg`); the `WeaponFlag` enum at chronicle:ps2/include/itemdata.hpp
- Status ailments on the player are bits in `UserStatus->ailments[6]`: knocked out 0x02, freeze 0x04, "stamina" 0x08, poison 0x10, curse 0x20, goo 0x40. The stamina bit is set by the Stamina Drink item and doubles melee and gun damage and the player's guard. chronicle:ps2/include/userstatus.hpp:29-35; chronicle:ps2/src/itembombeffect.cpp:121-123; chronicle:ps2/src/charakey.cpp:102, 660, 964
- Monster-side status hits use per-hit bits `flags` on the record: `0x100` stop, `0x200` poison, `0x800` slow, and `0x1000` (on a monster-owned hit that lands on another monster) provoke anger. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1218-1230, 1422-1460
- Player-side hit bits on monster attacks: `0x40000` money drain (20% roll, takes one fifth), `0x80000` halves max HP, `0x100000` freeze, `0x100` freeze unless an Anti-Freeze Amulet is held, `0x200` poison unless Antidote Amulet, `0x400` curse unless Anticurse Amulet, `0x800` goo unless Antigoo Amulet, `0x1000` stamina (buff-like; see Risks). chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7170-7300
- Monster hits on the player are split by `NowColData->hit[].kind`: kind 3 is a blow that pushes the player and, on the ordinary path, sets `recoil_frames = 0xA0` (160 steps); kinds 2 and 4 are heavier hits that drop the player unless the stick is held hard. The guard-like case is `BtActStatus.action_no == 9`, which turns a kind-3 hit into a sound and a consumed hit. chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7296-7345 (kind 3), 7352-7425 (kinds 2 and 4)
- The monster script entry points are 50 (reveal from chest), 100 (idle), 110 (hit reaction) and 120 (killed). chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1976-2002 (the switch on `CheckDmg`'s result)
- Monster-facing script opcodes that define combat: `_SET_BODY_COL` (130, hurt sphere on a named frame), `_SET_BODY_COL_PARA` (134, per-sphere parameters; indices 0-9 body, 10-15 per-attacker percentages), `_SET_DMG_COL` (131, attack sphere on a frame with start and end motion frames), `_SET_DMG_PARA` (132, damage, flags, kind, angle), `_SET_SHOT` (133) and `_SET_SHOT2` (229) for projectiles, `_SET_GUARD_FRAME` (244) and `_GUARD_SEARCH` (245), `_STATUS_SET_MUTEKI` (101) for invincibility, `_STATUS_SET_DEAD` (104). chronicle:ps2/src/runscript_opcodes.cpp:732-945, 1441-1494, 1633-1677
- Thirty-five projectile effect descriptors are monster shots, listed in `BtEntryEffectTbl[35]` from the `monstorunit_effect_data.inc` include. chronicle:ps2/include/monstorunit.hpp:589; chronicle:ps2/include/monstorunit_effect_data.inc:1-70
- The player's ranged and projectile objects: `CSHOT` (12 shots, 120-step life for Xiao's pellets) (unverified), `CSHOT_EFFECT` (8 slots, template-driven, the monster and special-attack shots), `CSHOT_MACHINGUN` (16 slots, Osmond's gun, 240-step age cap), `CSHOT_FIREBAR` (Osmond's flame), `MasekiEffect[5]` (item shots). chronicle:ps2/include/shot_effect.hpp:18-298; chronicle:ps2/src/shot_effect.cpp:54, 126, 626, 655; chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:445, 570-576
- Osmond's machine-gun hits are stamped with `owner = 5, attack_no = 6`, which the damage code treats specially (no hit-reaction script for non-boss monsters). chronicle:ps2/src/shot_effect.cpp:689-691; chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1344-1346
- Melee and ranged damage numbers are computed at the call sites. Toan's combo uses the weapon's `attack` with multipliers 1.8 (a later swing, frames 862-866) and 1.5 (two special moves, frames 193-196 and 720-722). Ruby's shots use `attack/2 + magic/2` (unverified). Osmond's guns use either `attack` or the same half-and-half formula, depending on gun style. Stamina doubles all of them. chronicle:ps2/src/charakey.cpp:98-170; chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7833-7850, 8118-8122, 8219-8223, 8297-8301
- Xiao's shots (`BattleActionPlay_Jinn`) fire from `CSHOT`, whose step stamps owner 1 and attack 0. The damage code reports `MONSTER_DMG_NONE` for owner 1, so the monster does not play its hit reaction (script 110) unless it dies. chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7655-7820; chronicle:ps2/src/shot_effect.cpp:70-71; chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1348-1350
- Kills: experience goes to the equipped weapon of the currently controlled character, and only if that character landed the killing blow. Default weapons earn none. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:2170-2196
- Weapon level-up (menu-side) computes attack and endurance gains from attachments in `WeaponLevelUpValueCalc`. The only caller passes `levels = 1`. chronicle:ps2/src/weaponlevelup.cpp:80-198; chronicle:ps2/src/menu_misc.cpp:395
- The event battle ("ebattle") is a timed button-prompt sequence, not a melee system. Scripts load it, set a motion and a list of keys with timings, run it frame by frame and read a result code. chronicle:ps2/src/ebattle.cpp:413-752; chronicle:ps2/src/editloop3.cpp:7785-7825
- The port overrides `EBLoop` only to add a config option that always reports success: `game.qte_always_win`. chronicle:port/src/ebattle.cpp:55, 161, 183; chronicle:port/src/platform/config.cpp:405

## How it works

### 1. The player's attack records

A player attack is a script-like state machine per character. The dungeon loop dispatches on `UserStatus->cur_chara` into the per-character handler at press time (`ToanKey_On`, `BattleActionOn_Jinn`, and so on) and again every frame while the action runs (`*_Play`). chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:3675-3730; chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:5099-5140 (the same for the second hand model)

Each frame of a melee swing that is inside a hit window creates a collision record:

1. The handler reads the frame position of a named hit frame (`dcol1` for Toan, `dcol0` for Osmond). Ruby's shots start from `weapon` and Xiao's from `eff30` on the hand model.
2. It calls `NowColData->Set(position, damage, life=1, radius, 0, target_mask=2, kind=2, flags=0, attribute=0)`. The radius is per hit: for Toan the sequence is 2.8, 5.3, 6.2, 6.2, 6.2, 6.0 and 12.0 world units.
3. `basic_damage` stamps the owner, attack number, `vs_monster`, `weapon_flags`, and the element bit in `flags`, replacing any flags the call passed.
4. `SetKickBack` sets the knockback origin, speed and decay, with mode 2 meaning the hit pushes what it strikes.

Shots (`CSHOT`, `CSHOT_EFFECT`, `CSHOT_MACHINGUN`, `CSHOT_FIREBAR`) do the same job on their own motion. Each step they test their position against monsters; on a hit they call `Set` with the shot's damage and then stamp owner and weapon data. `CSHOT_MACHINGUN` and `CSHOT_FIREBAR` set owner 5 and attack 6. chronicle:ps2/src/shot_effect.cpp:54-80, 126-291, 655-692; chronicle:ps2/src/shot_firebar.cpp:91-92

Item bombs and scripted explosions go through `SetBombEffect`, whose second argument is the target mask (1 player, 2 monsters, 3 both; the signature calls it `owner`, but it is passed to `Set` as `target_mask`) and which leaves the record's owner at -1 (no character). chronicle:ps2/src/itembombeffect.cpp:147-175. This is why bombs earn no experience and count as "item" damage (step 13 of section 2 and step 4 of section 4).

### 2. Monster damage: `CheckDmg`

`CheckDmg` runs once per monster per step from `CMonstorUnit::Step`, before the monster's script (chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1976). In order:

1. **Monster-owned hits.** Each active monster sphere in `effect2` (attack spheres) that is inside its motion window creates a hit on the player, with its damage doubled if the monster is angry. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1063-1120
2. **Dead or stopped.** A dead monster returns none. A stopped monster counts down its stop timer but can still be hit; stop only freezes its motion and script. Poison ticks here: when the poison timer runs out it removes 10% of max HP and rearms to 180 steps, so poison keeps ticking until a stop or anger hit clears it. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1120-1186
3. **Invincibility.** If `invincible_timer > 0`, return without hits. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1189-1191
4. **Find the sphere.** For each hurt sphere (`effect`) whose motion window is open, look for an active hit whose radius overlaps. Ignore hits from the same monster; hits owned by another monster provoke anger (`0x1000`) with `status_chance` odds and are rejected. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1196-1230
5. **Guard.** If one of the monster's up to three guard windows is open and the hit is a knockback-mode hit, the hit is rejected with a guard mark and melee durability loss at 0.1. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1236-1266
6. **Record the hit.** The hit's weapon ability bits go into `hit_attributes`, the owner into `last_attacker` on death, and the element comes from the hit's `flags` (default case: none, index 5). chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1278-1320. Steal chance (`0x80`, 10% roll, only if the monster has a steal item and the player does not already have it) is checked here too.
7. **Status bits.** Poison (`0x20`) needs a 10% roll and then a second roll under the status chance; stop (`0x40`) needs a 4% roll and then the same second check. For `0x200`, `0x100` and `0x800` the code compares a value it has just overwritten (see Risks). chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1397-1460
8. **Base damage.** Start from the hit's `damage` field. If owner is 1, 3 or 5 (Xiao, Ruby, Osmond), multiply by 1.5 within 20 units of the player and scale down linearly beyond 50 units, to a floor of 0.5 at 100 units. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1464-1476. Then subtract the monster's defense, with owner 3 (Ruby) halving the defense first. Clamp to at least 1. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1486-1496
9. **Elements.** If the hit has any element or status bit set, add a weapon bonus for player-owned hits, `damage * (0.005*magic + 0.004*elem[element])`, then multiply by the monster's weight for that element as a percentage. A zero result with a nonzero input marks the hit immune. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1498-1512
10. **Monster effectiveness.** Add `1.5%` per point of the weapon's `vs_monster` entry for the monster's family (signed bytes, so a negative entry reduces damage). chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1514-1520
11. **Per-attacker percentage.** For player-owned hits, multiply by the sphere's `parameter[sphere][owner]` percentage. Default 100. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1521-1531
12. **Target kind.** If the hit names a family (`target_kind`, set by shot effects through `SetEnemyAttr`) and the monster's family does not match, damage is zero and immune. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1538-1542
13. **Anger and items.** Anger halves damage taken. Owner -1 (bombs, thrown items, traps) multiplies by `item_damage_rate`. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1544-1550
14. **Rounding and rare effects.** The float damage is rounded up to an integer. Drain (`0x400`) heals the owner by 1% of damage when damage is at least 100 (unverified). Critical (`0x1000`) has a 1% chance to set the amount to the monster's remaining HP, except for no-lock-on kinds. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1555-1578
15. **Apply.** Subtract from HP. On death, set `last_attacker`, decrement the alive count, count the kill for the floor and set `event_flag2_pending` if the monster has a linked event. Non-immune hits flash the monster red, play the monster-hit sound and show a damage number; immune hits flash white. Non-"sticky" hits (`target_mask != 3`) are deactivated. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1580-1625
16. **Return.** Hit, killed, or none. The step then runs script 110 (hit) or 120 (killed). chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1976-1986

### 3. Player damage: `BtCheckDamageProc`

The player's check runs once per frame from the dungeon loop, outside the monster code. It uses `CheckHitUser` with the player's position and `CharaHeight`, skips the check while recoil or invincibility frames run, and applies:

1. Subtract `defense[cur_chara]`, doubled under the stamina bit, clamping at zero. chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7140-7147
2. Roll 0 to 99 once and reuse the roll for every ailment (a 65% check for most, a 20% check for money drain). Each bit either applies its ailment or is absorbed by a matching amulet, which is consumed on use. chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7170-7281
3. Halving (`0x80000`) replaces damage with half of max HP. chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7293
4. Kind 3 (blow) hits: if the player is in action 9, the hit only plays a sound and is consumed. Otherwise damage is applied, the blow velocity is set, `recoil_frames` becomes 160, and the player plays motion 6 (flinch) if they survive or motion 4 (knockdown) if the hit is fatal. chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7296-7345
5. Kind 2 and 4 hits: a hit that lands while the player is in action 6 only pushes them away. Otherwise damage is applied with a heavy damage number, and the player is knocked down (motion 4) unless the stick is held hard, in which case motion 0x1D is used. chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7352-7425
6. `AddNowLife(cur_chara, -damage)` and a damage number over the character's head at `CharaHeight`. chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7319

The port's override of this function (`BtCheckDamageProc`, chronicle:port/src/dun/movechara.cpp:3012) keeps the same order of checks: guard doubling and subtraction (port lines 3068-3077), the 20% money drain (3102), the half-HP rule (3225), and the life subtraction (3251, 3324). The Ixion-relevant point is that the logic exists twice, once in `ps2/src` and once in the port, so a change has to land in both or in the override.

### 4. Monster status and reward

Status timers are per monster, in steps (one per game update):

- Poison: 180-step rearm, 10% of max HP per tick; cleared by a stop hit or an anger provoke.
- Stop: 300 steps; a second stop hit cancels it. Clears poison, slow and anger.
- Slow: 180 steps, applied only when the monster is not stopped, poisoned or angry. The header describes it as halving motion and movement speed. chronicle:ps2/include/monstorunit.hpp:69
- Anger: 1800 steps on a provoke; 180 steps refreshed every step on back-dungeon monsters, which the header describes as "stay angry". Anger doubles the damage a monster deals and halves the damage it takes. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1224, 1947, 1076, 1544

On death (`state == -1`), `Step` handles the rewards once: experience to the current character's equipped weapon (only if it is the killer, not a default weapon, and not in a no-weapon-change zone); the weapon-ability `0x10` drink; the stolen-money drop; then the item drop. Keys and rare items are offered through `SetGateKeyStack`, which refuses an item already on the stack, so a repeat drop of the same key does nothing. Money drops only when no item was chosen. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:2170-2268; chronicle:ps2/src/collisiondata.cpp:758-776

Attachment drops come from `SelectAttachi`: about 71% of kills roll an attachment; within that, 30% pick the monster's strongest elemental attachment and the rest pick the slayer attachment for the monster's family. The attachment is actually dropped only if `last_attacker == -1`, which means the monster died to an unowned hit (see Risks). chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1022-1058, 2241

### 5. Monster runtime

- **Setup.** `SetupViewMonstor` takes a model index, runs the species script once (`run(1)`), copies the table's stats into `MONSTOR`, and marks the spawn as revealed or hidden (family 8, the mimic family, starts hidden and calls `SetMimicEvent` with the small or large box kind). chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:2435-2500
- **Per-step motion and collision.** `Step` moves the monster along its movement vector, checks walls and the player (`MoveCheck`, `MoveCheck2`) and other monsters (`MoveChecMonster`), and only then runs `CheckDmg`. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1854-2010
- **Attack projectiles.** `_SET_SHOT` and `_SET_SHOT2` attach a projectile to a named frame; `Step` fires it through `NowShotEffect->Set`, optionally overriding its damage. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:2130-2155
- **Palette and sound.** Each hit starts a blend towards red (or white for an immune hit), and the palette cycle and per-motion sound windows run in `PalletStep` and `SoundCheck`. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:636-739
- **Shadows and drawing.** `DrawMonstor` draws the monsters in play. Dormant monsters within detail distance draw as well (port override). chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:739-815; chronicle:port/src/monstorunit.cpp:15-40

### 6. Event battle (ebattle)

This is a separate timed-prompt sequence. A script calls the init opcode (`_EB_INIT`, which loads two textures and sets speed to four times a multiplier), then sets a motion list (`_EB_SET_MOTION`), then adds timed keys (`_EB_SET_KEY`: frame time, button mask, early-highlight mode capped at 5). `_EB_LOOP` returns the result once per frame. chronicle:ps2/src/editloop3.cpp:7785-7825; chronicle:ps2/src/ebattle.cpp:413-523

Each key has a window that opens 16 frames before its frame and closes 48 frames after it (scaled by the speed). A press inside the window with the right buttons counts as a hit; "cool" timing is within 24 frames after the key's frame. A wrong press or a missed key ends it with a failure code, which fades the music and returns after 80 frames. A full clear returns 1, or 2 if every key was cool. chronicle:ps2/src/ebattle.cpp:610-752

The result code `1` is also what `EBLoop` returns when no event battle is running, so a script cannot tell "not started" from "cleared normally" by that return alone. chronicle:ps2/src/ebattle.cpp:610-613

## Ixion hooks

These are the places a DC2 character or monster would have to touch. Each names the function and what Ixion could do there. All are retail-code locations unless marked "port".

**Player side**

- `LoadChara2` (chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:6871) and `LoadWeapon2` (chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:6609): the single entry for swapping the model, motions and equipped weapon. An Ixion character would be a new model pack loaded into the current slot, keeping `cur_chara` as the owner index.
- The action dispatch at chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:3675-3730 and 5099-5140: where a new move set would be selected. Adding a case here is the minimal change; replacing a whole handler is possible because `ToanKey_On` and friends are file-scope, non-static functions and can be given a `PORT_OVERRIDE` body (port only).
- `basic_damage` (chronicle:ps2/src/charakey.cpp:39): one place that stamps owner, attack number, `vs_monster`, `weapon_flags` and element for melee. A DC2 weapon's stat block would be converted into `WEAPON_HAVE` fields here or at the same point in a port override.
- `BtCheckDamageProc` (port: chronicle:port/src/dun/movechara.cpp:3012): where incoming damage, defense, ailments and knockback are applied to the player. A DC2 defense or resistance scheme would live here, and `CharaHeight` (chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:52) would need a seventh entry or a lookup.
- `WEAPON_HAVE` and `CUserStatus` (chronicle:ps2/include/itemdata.hpp:686-704; chronicle:ps2/include/userstatus.hpp:88-127): the attack, magic, element and monster-effectiveness data a player weapon carries. Any DC2 weapon has to be expressed in these fields.

**Monster side**

- `MonstorTable` (port override at chronicle:port/src/monstorunit.cpp:44): the species table. Adding a monster means adding a row, changing the array size in the header (chronicle:ps2/include/monstorunit.hpp:586) and the port table, and adding `dun/monstor/<name>.chr` plus a script. Every row must keep its `monster_no` as an index.
- `BtEnemyLayoutList` and `BtUraEnemyLayoutList` (chronicle:ps2/include/monstorunit_floor_data.inc): the per-floor species lists. Only the `monster_no` field matters today, so an Ixion monster on a DC1 floor is one row edit.
- `CMonstorUnit::ArrangementPos` (chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:550) and `SetupViewMonstor` (chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:2435): spawn and stat setup. A port override here could scale HP, defense or weights for Ixion difficulty, or add a second table of species for crossover floors.
- `CMonstorUnit::CheckDmg` (chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1060): not overridden today. A port override could give a DC2 element or status class its own branch by owner, as long as owner ids stay in the existing ranges or the arrays are widened.
- Script opcodes `_SET_BODY_COL` (130), `_SET_DMG_COL` (131), `_SET_DMG_PARA` (132), `_SET_SHOT` (133), `_SET_BODY_COL_PARA` (134) (chronicle:ps2/src/runscript_opcodes.cpp:732-945): the authoring API for hurt spheres, attack spheres, projectiles and per-attacker percentages. A DC2 monster's attacks need one attack sphere per hit frame.
- `CMonstorUnit::Step` death branch (chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:2170-2268): reward and drop logic. A cross-world reward rule would go here.

**Shared**

- `CCollisionData::Set` (chronicle:ps2/src/collisiondata.cpp:797-829): add a port override that returns -1 when the pool is full, so every caller sees a real "no hit" result (port only). This is the cheapest safety fix for Ixion, since it adds hits.
- `ebattle` (`EBLoop`, port: chronicle:port/src/ebattle.cpp:55): a QTE step for a crossover finisher. Keys come from `_EB_SET_KEY`. `game.qte_always_win` (chronicle:port/src/platform/config.cpp:405) already gives a test switch.
- `HitValueEntry` (chronicle:ps2/src/hitvalue.cpp; `HIT_VALUE_*` kinds at chronicle:ps2/include/hitvalue.hpp:14-19): damage numbers. A new kind for DC2 damage would need a digit sheet.

## Risks and blockers

1. **Six party slots are hard-coded everywhere the damage path looks.** Owner indices are array subscripts (`chara_weapons[owner]`, `parameter[sphere][owner]`, `CharaHeight[6]`, `defWeapon__2[6]`, `UserStatus->hp[6]`), and the save layout stores six slots. A seventh character would overflow several of them. Mapping a DC2 character to an existing slot is the practical route. See `c1-characters.md`.
2. **Per-character attack timing is hard-coded in frame numbers.** Toan's windows are literal motion frames (for example 825 to 828, 862 to 866 in `ToanKey_Play`), and some timing windows are split by `PAL_TIMING` (for example in `BattleActionPlay_Jinn`). Authoring a DC2 move means writing the window per move, not in data. chronicle:ps2/src/charakey.cpp:98-170; chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7749
3. **Collision pool overflow is undefined behaviour.** `CCollisionData::Set` has no return on the full path. Any caller that adds hits while 96 are active gets an indeterminate result, and the port compiles this with a modern compiler that can treat the path as unreachable. chronicle:ps2/src/collisiondata.cpp:797-829
4. **Out-of-range read on element-less status hits.** When a hit has any `flags` bit set but no element bit (for example a thrown Gooey Peach with `0x800`, Throbbing Cherry `0x100`, Poisonous Apple `0x200`, all with owner -1), the element switch defaults to index 5. The damage code then multiplies by `attachment_weight[5]`, one past the end of its array. In the PS2 layout that slot is the low half of the monster's `money` field, so the multiplier depends on the monster's money value. (The companion read, `weapon->elem[5]`, only runs for player-owned hits, so it does not occur for thrown items.) This is inference from struct layout, and the port inherits it because `CheckDmg` is not overridden. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1498-1509; chronicle:ps2/src/mainitemmodel.cpp:215-227; chronicle:ps2/include/monstorunit.hpp:63-100 (struct `MONSTOR`)
5. **Status chance is flattened for three of the status bits.** For `0x100`, `0x200` and `0x800`, the code overwrites the random roll with `chance = status_chance == 0 ? 100 : 0`, so any nonzero status chance always applies and zero never does. The percentage is effectively a boolean for those bits. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1422-1460
6. **One bit, two meanings.** The same hit-flag bit means different things by target. `0x100` is freeze when it hits the player and stop when it hits a monster. `0x1000` is provoke-anger on monster-owned hits and a stamina status on player-side handling; the stamina status is the same one the Stamina Drink sets, so under the current code a monster hit with that bit appears to buff the player. Any Ixion bridge that reuses these bits has to pick a side. chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7187; chronicle:ps2/src/itembombeffect.cpp:121-123
7. **Stamina naming is inconsistent.** Several comments in the damage and action code say "a poisoned weapon hits twice as hard" for the `AILMENT_STAMINA` bit, while the enum and item code treat it as a power-up. The behaviour (double damage and double guard) is clear from code; the name is not. chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7672, 7840, 8120, 8221, 8299 (the comment); chronicle:ps2/src/charakey.cpp:102; chronicle:ps2/src/dun/gameloop.cpp:7140-7142
8. **Table columns that look live but may not be.** Two columns are unread or overwritten. The layout's first and third fields are never read (see Key facts). The species table's `effect_parameter` percentages are copied into every hurt-sphere slot at spawn (`SetupViewMonstor`), but `_SET_BODY_COL` resets that slot's six percentages to 100 when a script defines the sphere. Since scripts normally define their spheres at spawn, the table column looks dead for most monsters (inference). The other way to set per-attacker values is opcode 134 with parameters 10 to 15. Whether any shipped script uses 134 needs the decoded `.stb` files. chronicle:ps2/src/runscript_opcodes.cpp:757-789; chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:2502
9. **Repeat hits from non-consumed records.** A hit with `target_mask == 3` (item bombs, some shot effects) is not deactivated after it hits. Its repeat damage per monster is therefore limited only by its life or by a script's invincibility timer. This is inference from `CheckDmg`; the scripts that set invincibility were not checked. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1614; chronicle:ps2/src/itembombeffect.cpp:157
10. **Opcode table hang.** `BtSetEventExtendTable` spins forever (`while (1)`) if two entries share an opcode number. Adding Ixion opcodes must keep numbers unique. chronicle:ps2/src/runscript_opcodes.cpp:1707
11. **Debug output on the hot path.** `CheckDmg` prints the element and distance factor on every hit (`printf("element = %d")`, `printf("c_dist = ...")`), and the shot opcodes print too. This is harmless on PS2 but slows and floods the port's console when many shots land. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1293, 1469, 1481; chronicle:ps2/src/runscript_opcodes.cpp:916
12. **Latent level-up loop bug.** `WeaponLevelUpValueCalc` reuses `i` for the inner attachment loop, so with `levels > 1` only one level is applied. The only caller passes 1, so it is latent. chronicle:ps2/src/weaponlevelup.cpp:85-96; chronicle:ps2/src/menu_misc.cpp:395
13. **Attachment drops may not match the wiki.** The attachment branch runs only when `last_attacker == -1`, so attachments drop from kills by bombs, thrown items and traps, not from weapon kills. The community wiki says monsters drop attachments commonly. Either the wiki or the code is out of date; this needs an in-game check. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:2241; community wiki page "Attachments" (pulled copy)
14. **Boss behaviour is keyed to map and floor numbers.** The boss-only rules (the Divine Beast Cave floor 14, where Dran sits, and the Gallery of Time floor 24, where the Dark Genie sits) are literal constants in `CheckDmg`. Moving or copying those floors in Ixion breaks them. chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:1333-1341
15. **Weapon ability bit names disagree.** The `WeaponFlag` enum calls `0x10` "increases thirst drain", but the kill path grants 10 drink for `0x10`. The community pages were not checked for a drink effect. Treat the code as the truth and the enum as unverified until checked. chronicle:ps2/include/itemdata.hpp; chronicle:ps2/src/monstorunit.cpp:2212
16. **Event battle returns are ambiguous.** `EBLoop` returns 1 both when idle and on a normal clear. Scripts that test the result have to know the state from elsewhere. chronicle:ps2/src/ebattle.cpp:610-613
17. **Shot hit recording depends on pool space.** Shot step code calls `Set` without checking the result and then writes into `hit[now_hit]`. On a full pool `now_hit` still names the most recent record, which may still be active, so the write can alter a live hit. chronicle:ps2/src/shot_effect.cpp:69-75, 179-186

## Open questions

- Which event scripts call the event battle, and how many story fights use it? Needs the decoded event scripts (not in the DCExtractor specifications).
- The monster script format (`dun/monstor/*.stb`) is not in `refs/DCExtractor/Specifications`. Authoring a DC2 monster needs that format, or the monster AI has to be written in port code.
- Whether any shipped monster script calls `_SET_BODY_COL_PARA` with parameters 10 to 15 (making the table's per-attacker percentages live) or relies on the 100 default.
- Whether the dungeon loop ever reads the layout's first and third fields through a path other than `BtLoadMonstor`. Only that function was found.
- Step rate of the PAL build (the port's tick rate and the PAL timing variants are assumed, not measured).
- Whether the scripts that run on hit (110) set `invincible_timer` for hits that are not consumed, which decides how often a bomb hits the same monster.
- The meaning of weapon ability bit `0x2000` (experience times 1.2 in the code) and whether the `0x10` kill drink is the intended behaviour.
- Whether the attachment-only-on-unowned-kill rule is intended. It needs an in-game test on a monster that the wiki says drops attachments.
- Whether `SelectAttachi`'s elemental choice ever fires: it depends on `changed`, which needs a monster whose strongest weight is not index 0.
